Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence simulates human intelligence in machines, enabling them to think, learn, and make decisions.

What is AI?

Intelligent Machines

AI mimics human cognitive functions like learning, reasoning, and problem-solving.

Goal: Create systems that think and act rationally.
Types: Narrow AI, General AI, Super AI

Expert Systems

Knowledge-Based

Computer systems that emulate decision-making ability of human experts.

Tech: Rules Engine, Inference Engine
Use: Medical Diagnosis, Financial Analysis

Natural Language Processing

Language Understanding

Enables machines to understand and respond to human language.

Tech: Transformers, BERT, GPT
Use: Chatbots, Voice Assistants, Translation

Computer Vision

Visual Perception

AI that extracts information from images and videos.

Tech: OpenCV, YOLO, ResNet
Use: Face Recognition, Object Detection, OCR

Reinforcement Learning

Reward-Based Learning

AI learns by interacting with environment and receiving rewards/punishments.

Tech: Q-Learning, Deep Q Networks
Use: Game AI, Robotics, Autonomous Vehicles

Responsible AI

Ethical AI

Developing AI that is fair, transparent, and accountable.

Focus: Bias Mitigation, Explainability
Use: Ethical Frameworks, AI Governance
